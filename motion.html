Text

Type that moves with intent

Each effect below is a from-scratch re-implementation of an OriginKit component, stripped to what fits an editorial portfolio. Hover, scroll and resize to exercise them. Anything can be switched on with data-motion="name" or off for a whole subtree with data-motion="off".

Words diffuse in one at a time, like smoke settling — and blur back out when they scroll away.

Smoky Text → word-level reveal · auto on h2 inside .reveal · opt in: data-motion="words" on any .reveal

Weight follows the cursor

Dynamic Weight → per-letter variable-font weight by cursor distance · auto on .next-project h2 · opt in: data-motion="weight"

Decoded on load

Scramble Text → cinematic glyph decode, left to right with jitter · auto on .cs-hero h1 · opt in: data-motion="scramble"

Enter fromabove orbelow

Direction Hover → accent copy slides in from the edge the cursor enters, leaves through the edge it exits · auto on nav + footer links · opt in: data-motion="dh"

Endless sheen sweep

Shiny Pill → CSS-only text sheen · auto on .kicker, .sec-kicker · works on the encrypted homepage too

Images

Reveals that earn the wait

Pixel Reveal + press tilt → grid of squares dissolves with a glowing ragged edge, then the cover tilts under the cursor · auto on .cs-cover, .full-img, .gallery img · opt in: data-motion="pixel tilt"

Pixel Reveal → sweep direction alternates (right, down, left, up) per image so a gallery never reveals in lockstep

Surfaces

Cards that answer the cursor

250+components in the OriginKit library used as reference
10effects re-implemented here without a single dependency
4.6/5decimals and suffixes survive the count-up intact
1,200thousands separators too

Spotlight → cursor-following radial glow · auto on .stat, .persona, .kqs-card, .cs-meta > div, .callout · opt in: data-motion="spot"
Count-up → numbers tick up on first view · auto on .stat b · opt in: data-motion="count"

Callouts get the same spotlight. Everything here also works with keyboard focus and on touch, where the cursor effects simply stay quiet.

Buttons

Pull, then let go

Primary button Ghost button Nav pill

Magnetic → buttons lean toward the cursor and spring back on leave · Sheen → CSS-only light sweep every few seconds · auto on .btn, .btn-ghost, .nav-cta · opt in: data-motion="magnetic"

Backdrop

Always on, never loud

Aurora drift — three soft radial glows drift behind every page on a 34-second loop (CSS-only, so the homepage has it too).

Blinking Squares — the hero grid twinkles at 30fps, thins out toward the bottom, brightens in a halo around the cursor, and pauses when scrolled away or when the tab is hidden. Canvas resolution is capped at 1.5× so it stays cheap on high-DPI handhelds.

Scroll hint — a pulse travels down the hero's hairline instead of a static gradient.

  • Opt out per subtree: data-motion="off"
  • Reduced-motion users get the final state of everything immediately
  • Touch devices skip the cursor-driven effects and keep the scroll-driven ones
